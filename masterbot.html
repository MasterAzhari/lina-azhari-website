FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. MasterBot connects to your existing number through WhatsApp's "Linked devices".

Will the AI make things up?

The AI only answers from the information you give it, and you can test everything in the Playground before going live.

Can my staff take over a chat?

Yes. When staff reply, the bot pauses for that chat. Press "Turn bot on" to let it continue.

Does it work for agent teams?

Yes. It's built for teams: shared inbox, handover alerts, and leaders can copy settings to every agent.

Can it send pictures and videos?

Yes. It sends images, posters and YouTube links with a preview.
