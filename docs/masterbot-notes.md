## Headline and value (translated from Malay)
- Badge: "AI WhatsApp for agents & sellers"
- Headline: "Lead comes in at midnight? MasterBot answers first." (BM: "Lead masuk tengah malam? MasterBot jawab dulu.")
- Value: MasterBot replies to WhatsApp customers within seconds, sends prices, product photos and videos, then hands over to staff when the customer is ready to buy. No more cold leads from slow replies.
- Points: uses your existing WhatsApp number · replies in casual Malay · staff can take over any time.

## Problem it solves
Ads are paid for, but leads are lost to slow replies: customers who click an ad ask 3-4 sellers at once, and whoever answers first wins. Messages arrive while you sleep; the same questions repeat (price, warranty, how to apply); big teams lose track of who is serving which customer.

## Features
- **AI answers 24/7** based on your product info, in the tone you set.
- **Keyword auto-reply**: e.g. "I want aircond info" sends the price, poster and YouTube video. Good for ad leads.
- **Hand over to staff**: when a form is filled or a human is requested, the bot stops and staff get a WhatsApp notification.
- **Team inbox**: all chats on one screen (Active, Needs staff, Resolved); staff reply with images, video or documents.
- **Automatic follow-up** for quiet leads on your schedule.
- **Dashboard**: new leads, waiting for staff, replies by AI / keyword / human.
- **Playground**: test the bot before going live; nothing is sent.
- **Copy settings to agents**: a leader copies a complete bot template to every agent.
- **Connect by QR**: scan from WhatsApp on the phone; panel works on laptop and phone.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. We set it up for you: workspace, product info, prices and sales script.
2. Scan the WhatsApp QR code to connect your number (about a minute).
3. The bot looks after leads, you close the sale.
