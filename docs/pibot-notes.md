# Pibot: notes for the product page

Sources read 2026-11-08: pibot.io, viewer.pibot.io, viewer.pibot.io/main (live bot), builder.pibot.io (Azhari's logged-in builder, browser 1).

## What Pibot is
- Visual chatbot builder hosted by LAM. pibot.io headline: **"Build advanced chatbots visually"**.
- Tagline: "PiBot gives you powerful blocks to create unique chat experiences. Embed them anywhere on your web/mobile apps and start collecting results like magic."
- Feature list on pibot.io: Easy building experience · Embed it in a click · Integrate with any platform · Collect results in real-time.
- viewer.pibot.io: no-code platform to create and integrate advanced chatbots into websites and chat platforms like WhatsApp.
- Builder (builder.pibot.io): drag-and-drop flow editor. Bubbles (text, image, video, embed, audio), inputs (text, number, email, website, date, time, phone, buttons, and more), Theme, Settings, Share, Results tabs, Test and Publish buttons. Bots show a "Live" badge once published.
- Live bot (viewer.pibot.io/main, in Malay): "Selamat Datang ke ChatBot PiBoT", "Sila tekan butang pilihan di bawah ?" with buttons Log in · Daftar sini · Contact Support · Video Demo cara guna PiBOT.
- Real use: event registration bot with a welcome message, a greeting image, the "How would you like to be addressed?" question and Mr./Mrs. buttons.
- Who it's for: businesses, organisations and event organisers who want to capture leads, registrations and enquiries through chat on their website or a shared link.
- Pricing: none shown on any page. CTA on pibot.io: "JOIN HERE" / "ENTER"; live bot CTA: "Daftar sini" (sign up).

## Legal / licensing (must respect on the LAM page)
pibot.io states Pibot is a hosted, unmodified instance of the open-source **Typebot** (by Baptiste Arnaud, FSL licence), not affiliated with or endorsed by Typebot. Source: https://github.com/baptisteArno/typebot.io. The builder UI shows "Typebot" in page titles. Avoid claiming LAM built the engine; describe it as LAM's hosted chatbot service, and keep the attribution line on the page.
